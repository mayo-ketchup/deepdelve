# Deepdelve

An idle pixel-art RPG. Choose a Knight, Ranger or Mage and delve down through five realms, from the
Whispering Woods to the Outer Realm. Fight bosses, gather materials, craft your own gear and grow
stronger, even while you're away.

## Play

**https://mayo-ketchup.github.io/deepdelve/**

Plays in any modern browser on desktop or phone. No download or account needed.

**Install it on your phone:** open the link, then use your browser's **Add to Home Screen**
(on iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). It opens full-screen like an app and keeps working offline
after the first visit.

## Features

- **Three classes**: the sturdy Knight, the swift Ranger and the spell-casting Mage
- **Five realms** with their own monsters, bosses and zone guardians
- **Hero levels**: earn experience and choose how your hero grows
- **Loot and crafting**: find gear in the dungeon or forge your own at Hearthstead
- **Five gathering skills**: Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing, Herbalism and Tailoring
- **Cooking**: turn meat from the dungeon into meals that keep your hero going
- **Hearthstead**: a town to rest, trade and prepare between delves
- **Routines**: plan a loop of gathering, delving, cooking and crafting, and your hero follows it
- **Idle progress**: your hero keeps going while you're away

## Your save

Progress is saved automatically in your browser. Clearing your browser data, or playing in a
different browser or device, starts a new game.
